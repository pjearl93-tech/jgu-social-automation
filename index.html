Social Media Content Management

Manage and publish social content more efficiently.

JGU Social Automation is a content management tool designed to help authorized users organize, prepare and publish video content to their connected social media accounts.

About JGU Social Automation

JGU Social Automation supports the workflow from video content preparation to publishing. Users connect accounts they own or are authorized to manage and explicitly choose the content that will be published.

What we do

Content Management

Organize video content and prepare publishing information for connected social media channels.

Content Preparation

Prepare titles, descriptions and hashtags associated with video content before publishing.

TikTok Publishing

Authorized users can send selected video content to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Publishing Status

Track publishing requests and manage the status of content submitted to connected platforms.

User Control

JGU Social Automation does not publish content from unauthorized accounts. Users control which accounts are connected and which content is submitted for publishing.
