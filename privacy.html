Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

JGU Social Automation respects user privacy and processes information only as necessary to provide social media content management and publishing services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the services used, we may process:

  • Social media account identifiers, display names and usernames.
  • Authorization information and access tokens provided through official platform authorization processes.
  • Video content selected by users for publishing.
  • Content information such as titles, descriptions and hashtags.
  • Publishing results, status information and service logs.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate and connect authorized social media accounts.
  • Process user-requested video publishing operations.
  • Prepare and transmit publishing information.
  • Check publishing status and troubleshoot errors.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. TikTok Data

When a user connects TikTok, JGU Social Automation accesses TikTok data only through TikTok's official APIs and only within permissions authorized by the user. TikTok information is used to provide the requested account connection and content publishing functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to connected social media platforms when necessary to perform publishing operations explicitly requested by the user.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements. Users may request deletion of their stored information and may revoke platform permissions through the applicable social media platform.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. API credentials, authorization tokens and other sensitive credentials are not intended to be publicly disclosed.

7. User Rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion of applicable personal information and may disconnect or revoke access to connected social media accounts.

8. Third-Party Services

Use of connected platforms such as TikTok is also subject to the respective platform's terms, privacy policies and developer policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Operator: JGU
For privacy or data-related inquiries, please contact the JGU service administrator.
